/* ABOUT */

We are RareRoles — a specialized technical recruitment partner focused on hard-to-fill enterprise technology roles.

Mission: To bridge the gap between exceptional technical talent and companies that need them, focusing on rare, specialized skills that are difficult to source through traditional recruitment methods.

Our Story: Founded in 2024, RareRoles was born from a simple observation — the most critical technical roles take the longest to fill. Companies waste months searching for Oracle PL/SQL developers, CCIE network engineers, AIX administrators, and AI specialists. We built RareRoles to solve this problem with a pre-vetted talent pipeline, deep technical screening, and specialized industry expertise.
